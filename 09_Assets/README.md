# 09 — Assets

## Purpose
Non-text assets used across the FRKP — images, logos, diagrams (source and export), style files, fonts, and multimedia.

## Scope
- Diagrams (source .drawio, .excalidraw, .svg)
- Logos and branding assets
- Images and screenshots
- CSS/style templates for document generation
- Fonts and typography assets

## Relationship with other folders
| Folder | Relationship |
|---|---|
| All folders | Assets are referenced by content in any folder. |
| 08_Output | Rendered versions of diagrams may be placed in Output. |
| docs/ | Site assets may be symlinked or copied for build. |
