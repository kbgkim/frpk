# 99 — Archive

## Purpose
Historical snapshots of completed work, superseded documents, closed sprints, and deprecated content preserved for audit trail and reference.

## Scope
- Superseded versions of approved documents
- Closed sprint artifacts
- Deprecated policies and templates
- Historical meeting notes and reviews
- Any content no longer current but retained for traceability
