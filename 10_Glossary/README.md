## Purpose
Single source of truth for all terminology used across the FRKP. Every term has a unique ID, definition, context, and cross-references.

## Scope
- Financial risk terminology
- Abbreviations and acronyms
- Mathematical notation definitions
- Regulatory terms and definitions
- Cross-references to KB articles, formulas, and volumes

| Folder | Relationship |
