# 03 — Formula Catalog

## Purpose
Authoritative catalog of financial risk formulas, models, and quantitative methods. Each formula entry includes notation, assumptions, derivation notes, and usage guidance.

## Scope
- Risk metrics (VaR, CVaR, etc.)
- Pricing models (Black-Scholes, binomial trees, etc.)
- Statistical and econometric methods
- Regulatory capital formulas
- Notation conventions and symbol registry

| Folder | Relationship |
