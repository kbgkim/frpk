# 02 — Knowledge Base

## Purpose
Structured repository of financial risk knowledge articles, how-to guides, FAQs, and decision trees. The primary living knowledge asset of the platform.

## Scope
- Risk management concepts and frameworks
- How-to guides and standard operating procedures
- FAQs and troubleshooting guides
- Cross-referenced topic clusters

## Relationship with other folders
| Folder | Relationship |
|---|---|
| 03_Formula_Catalog | KB articles may reference formulas by ID. |
| 04_Glossary | All terms used in KB articles are defined in the Glossary. |
| 05_Volumes | Volumes provide the deep-dive treatment; KB provides summary/quick-reference. |
