## Purpose
Long-form, in-depth treatments of financial risk subjects. Volumes are the primary deliverable of the FRKP and represent approved, publishable content.

## Scope
- Comprehensive risk domain treatments (Market Risk, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, etc.)
- Volume structure: front matter, chapters, appendices, index
- Each volume follows a standard template defined in 00_Project_Management/Templates
- Versioned and subject to full document lifecycle (Draft → Review → Approved → Frozen → Published)
