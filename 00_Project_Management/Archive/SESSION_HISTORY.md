This document records the chronological history of all major FRKP development sessions.

It serves as the official development journal for the Financial Risk Knowledge Platform.

# 8. Lessons Learned

The Version 1.0 development established several important practices.

* Repository-first design improves long-term maintainability.
* Bundle-based organization scales well.
* Cross-reference validation is essential before publication.
* Knowledge Graph significantly improves navigation.
* AI collaboration is most effective when supported by clear governance and repository standards.
